# Music Directory for Our Love Story
Place your romantic Telugu audio files here!

The audio player is pre-configured to look for:
1. `intro.mp3` - The opening ambience / initial unlock track
2. `friendship.mp3` - Chapters I to III (Diploma & friendship years)
3. `simhachalam.mp3` - Chapter IV (The Hand I Didn't Want To Let Go)
4. `confession.mp3` - Chapter VI (15 March 2025 proposal / confession)
5. `memories.mp3` - Memory timeline and scrapbook
6. `final-letter.mp3` - Birthday reveal & Tarun's final love letter

* Note: The website includes an enchanted built-in romantic ambient piano melody engine that plays automatically if an MP3 file is not found, ensuring the experience is ALWAYS rich and cinematic!
